Frequently asked

How is Atlas different from ChatGPT?
Atlas is a domain-specific readiness system with rule packs, structured evidence, record-level validation, ownership, approvals, and implementation handoff.
Can my team run scans without me?
Yes. Atlas standardizes the method so qualified team members produce consistent outputs for your review.
How long does a scan take?
A limited teardown provides an early view of readiness within days. A complete engagement typically takes one to three weeks, depending on scope and complexity.
What about data security?
Data handling, access, retention, and NDA requirements are agreed during engagement setup.
