Validators show the rule. OneSig shows the fix.

Free validators tell you whether an invoice file passes. OneSig explains why the business transaction failed, which source field caused it, who needs to fix it, and how to preserve the evidence.

Example

Same invoice. Two answers.

Tax-amount mismatch on Line 3
Free validator says

PEPPOL-EN16931-R004: tax amount mismatch.

OneSig says

Line 3 rounds €0.01 below the invoice total. Change rounding mode to half-up and update the source ERP tax calculation.
