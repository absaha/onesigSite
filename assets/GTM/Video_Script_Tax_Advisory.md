# Video Script — Tax Advisory & Consulting Firms

*~3 minutes · Aug 2026 (current) · Audience: tax advisory / CA firms / consulting firms (the advisory ICP).*
*Spine: competitive-displacement hook (from the /for/advisors page headline) → expertise-is-a-capability diagnosis → discovery doesn't scale on partner hours → Atlas under their brand → follow-on program leadership → outcome-selling shift → whose-brand-is-on-the-answer close.*
*Notes: no Zimmer Biomet here — the advisor's fear is losing the client relationship to whoever answers first, so the whole script runs on competitive displacement. "Assessment" appears only as a description of what competitors peddle, never what we sell.*

---

Your clients are asking discovery questions right now. What does the mandate touch? Is our data ready? What has to change in the ERP?

And here's the uncomfortable truth: someone else is already offering to answer them. A Big 4 firm. A technology vendor. A platform with a "free assessment" button.

*(beat)*

The old answer to that threat was expertise. "We know tax. We know VAT. We've been doing this twenty years."

But expertise is a capability — and a capability gives your client something to compare, not a reason to stay. Every firm says it. And your clients can get a decent first answer from AI in thirty seconds now. Knowing things stopped being the differentiator.

What clients actually buy is a result: *tell me exactly what breaks, what it costs, who fixes it, and when I'm ready.* And the firm that answers that question first — with evidence, not opinion — leads the whole program. Everyone else becomes a subcontractor to it.

*(beat)*

Here's the problem with delivering that answer today: it doesn't scale.

Discovery is manual. Every engagement gets reinvented — new interviews, new workshops, a new deck. The quality depends on which partner runs it. And partner hours are the ceiling on the whole practice: to sell more, you either work more nights or hire ahead of revenue.

That's the job we do.

*(beat)*

Atlas is the tool. Your team is the face.

Our agents read what your client already has — the documents, the data, the ERP configuration — and answer most of the discovery questions before anyone books a meeting. The only questions that reach your client are the ones no document can answer. Those go on a call, run by your people, because those are the judgment calls — the thing your client actually pays you for.

What comes out is a defined discovery engagement, under your brand: what breaks, what it costs, who owns each fix, and when they're ready to move. Every scan runs the same way, whoever on your team runs it — so your partners review the work instead of executing it.

And a tested answer to "what has to change" isn't the end of an engagement. It's the beginning of the next six. The tax-rule design. The ERP remediation. The master-data correction. The vendor selection. The testing, the governance, the audit support. When you lead the discovery, you lead the program — before any technology vendor defines the requirements for you.

*(beat)*

Which adds up to the biggest shift of all: you stop selling hours and start selling a result.

Not a guarantee — you don't run your client's business, and they know it. Clarity. Instead of "our team will assess your situation," you say: "here's exactly what this engagement will tell you, and here's the evidence behind every finding."

And you can't just declare that — you have to earn it, by doing the same work, the same way, enough times to speak about results with confidence. That usually takes a firm a decade of discipline. On our platform it's structural: every scan runs the same codified way, every finding lands in the record. Ten clients in, you're not saying "trust our experience." You're saying "clients like you typically find this" — and showing the receipts.

That's what moves you, in your client's eyes, from a firm they hired to a partner they keep.

*(beat)*

Your clients are asking the discovery questions today. Someone is going to answer them.

The only question is whose brand is on the answer.
