# 1 · Vision and Exit Plan — What Abhi Said

## The founding brief
Abhi is building a readiness tool for tax transformation, working with **Lab Zero (Lab0)** for the LLM layer ("multi-deterministic answers for the qualitative answers") and a domain expert validating use cases, mappings, and the question bank. His framing:

- "The overall plan is how we are building the tool around readiness. What should be the IP that I create? What are my IPs?"
- "My goal here is to sell this product in two to three years from now… have an exit."
- "By 2030, there will be a huge mandate in the UK and other places… we want to be the leading tool around readiness in any tax transformation."
- "The IP can be sold to any of the big consulting or any other partners who are in this field by then."

He asked for a laid-out plan: what it should be, what the IP should be, how to build it over 2–3 years to an exit — preceded by deep research into the field.

## The deep research he commissioned
He ran the deep-research workflow on: tax-tech M&A 2020–2026 (Thomson Reuters/Pagero, Vertex/ecosio, Avalara, Sovos, Big 4 boutique acquisitions), precedents of small compliance-content/methodology companies being acquired, confirmed mandate timelines (UK HMRC, EU ViDA 2030/2035, Germany, Belgium, Poland, Spain, France), the competitive landscape for readiness/gap-assessment tooling specifically, and which forms of IP are defensible (rule content, benchmark data, question banks, AI extraction methods). Target buyers named: Big 4, tax-tech vendors (Vertex, Sovos, Avalara, Thomson Reuters), or an SI. Exit by 2029–2030 ahead of the UK/ViDA wave.

## Clarifications he gave on exit structure
- Sell the **Atlas IP only** (carve-out); SunTech remains the delivery entity.
- Dual-track posture; UK approach to be decided by the research.

## Expansion beyond e-invoicing
"Once we are successful with e-invoicing, extend it to **tax engine implementation**… and also to other regulations that are coming, such as **Pillar Two** [he said "Pillar 1," corrected to Pillar Two], which has the same basic concept."

The positioning he dictated: "This still helps not only with your readiness but also with ensuring these projects are done in the right way. These are technology projects, and without these elements as part of your preparation, you will always end up losing more money." He asked for the docs to be updated to include this two-year product build.
