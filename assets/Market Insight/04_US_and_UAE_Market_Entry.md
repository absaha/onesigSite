# 4 · US and UAE Market Entry — What Abhi Said

## The PwC call
"I have a call with my ex-colleague from Amazon, who is now a **director with PwC Tax**. One-hour call, more to just connect. The goal is also to **land OneSig in the US** and try to do other kinds of tax transformation projects, such as **Pillar Two and TP work**, because all of them have this discovery/readiness/gap assessment work. Give me how I should flow the conversation."

He then shared the last ~30 minutes of the call transcript for feedback to incorporate. Key content he relayed from it: the contact's transfer-pricing engine demo ("treat clients' data and transfer pricing complexity as a first-class input rather than an obstacle"); the contact's view that "the money is not made in assessments — the money is all made in the execution"; that IT services companies universally struggle with assessments taking too long and customers not wanting to pay for them; and Abhi's own articulation on the call: "that whole engagement … is one partner or director on top, one manager and three analysts — you don't need the three analysts; the grunt work of talking to people, getting answers, formatting — that all goes." The contact translated it as: "you are shortening time to revenue."
