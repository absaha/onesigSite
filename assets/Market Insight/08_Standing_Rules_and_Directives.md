# 8 · Standing Rules and Directives Abhi Established

These are the rules laid down across the session that govern all future output.

## Framing rules
- **No "killing" framing.** "We don't want to come from the concept of killing." Elevation, never elimination — punch the ritual, never the people.
- **Say "with Atlas," not "AI."** "Let's not say 'AI', say 'with Atlas' — not 'after AI', but 'after Atlas'."
- **Generalize beyond boutiques.** The ceiling constraint is "routes through very few experts," not "routes through the founder." "It should be for everyone."
- **Broader than e-invoicing.** Collateral and the product page talk about "any tax transformation program"; e-invoicing is the current focus, not the definition.
- **Productize the problem statement.** Move away from the slide-deck deliverable; the ever-evolving requirement record and tracked handoffs are the product.
- **Loss framing, not fear.** Features→consequences; help the buyer see the full cost of a risk they already face; Socratic questions over claims.

## Audience-specific messaging locks
- **Advisors:** never timeline compression — throughput ("how many they can take on with current capabilities"); pull-through revenue stays theirs; the record signs their expertise.
- **SIs/ERP partners:** fewer change orders + quicker time to revenue + no new teams to spin up.
- **ASPs:** the meter runs on live accounts; they earn when invoices move; never ask them to build a services team; never say "assessment" to ASPs.
- **Enterprises are not an outreach ICP.**

## Commercial rules
- Never publish prices in outreach.
- ASP pricing is per-account (banded), anchored to metering math — never per report; never revenue share.
- Fee-at-risk / outcome-staked terms require founder confirmation (Abhi + Kanishka) before publication.
- Positioning: product built and delivered in the US, entering UAE/Middle East. Quoted $5,000/engagement; the UAE contact pushed to ~$10,000.

## Honesty and confidentiality rules
- Never claim US client implementations until one is real.
- Never name competitors, partners, or clients in messages.
- Never disclose rule content, the question bank, or pack internals — partners see outputs and workflow, never machinery.
- Verify third-party stats against primary sources before public use.
- Big 4 employees: relationship-only (independence rules); cannot be named publicly or take equity.

## Vocabulary locks
- Severity: **Blocker / High / Flag**. Confidence: **CONFIRMED / REPORTED / GAP**.
- Rule IDs, run IDs, re-runs, named owners on every finding; internal epistemic states are never shown publicly.
- "Connected ≠ ready"; "believed ready vs evidenced ready"; "the gap does not change — the moment of discovery prices it."

## Working-relationship directives
- Lab0: light touch — a 6-month partnership test, not a contract negotiation. "Lab0 proposes; Atlas promotes."
- Copy style: no em-dashes in website copy; crisp language, minimal scrolling.
- The offer is kept separate from the product page.
- The Tax Tech 500 ranking is a demand signal to lead with insight, not a trophy to lead with.
