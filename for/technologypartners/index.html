What slows the team down

Implementation should not wait on discovery.

Before your team can build, configure, or process transactions, someone has to establish:

  • What the client actually needs
  • Whether their data is ready
  • Which requirements are unresolved
  • What systems and processes need to change
  • Who owns each blocker

Atlas identifies and structures the work before it becomes an onboarding delay or an implementation escalation.
